When to refer

  • Buy-sell / shareholder agreements that need a current calculated value
  • Succession, partner entrance or exit, and ownership transitions
  • Estate and gift planning support (coordinate with tax counsel)
  • Internal decision-making where a Calculation Report is appropriate
  • Situations that need a Conclusion of Value, litigation, or formal opinion — we help you refer out

How the process works

  1. You introduce AXIS to your client (email intro is enough).
  2. Scope & fee are confirmed in a quoted engagement letter before work begins.
  3. Documents & analysis proceed under agreed Calculation procedures.
  4. Draft to you first (when you are the referring advisor), then final Calculation Report in English.

What the client receives

  • Written Calculation of Value (Calculation Report) in English
  • Analyst walkthrough of the calculation result
  • Quoted fee — Calculation of Value typically $2,000–$3,500; Preliminary Value Assessment from $750
