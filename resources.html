Typically: 3–5 years of tax returns, financial statements, a current aged receivables/payables report, details on owner compensation and benefits, and any relevant legal agreements. We provide a detailed, tailored document request list at engagement kickoff.

For most internal, planning, and transactional purposes — yes. A Calculation of Value typically takes 2–3 weeks from receipt of complete data. Annual Updates take 1–2 weeks. Timelines can vary depending on business complexity and how quickly documents are provided.

Reports are delivered in English. Service is conducted bilingually — scoping calls, document requests, management interviews, and the analyst walkthrough all happen in English or Spanish, whichever your client prefers. English delivery keeps the report consistent with the NACVA standards and comparable-transaction sources it relies on, and is the form lenders, attorneys, and counterparties expect.

All engagements follow NACVA Professional Standards, consistent with AICPA SSVS-1 and USPAP. These standards define the scope, methodology, documentation, and reporting requirements for every engagement.
