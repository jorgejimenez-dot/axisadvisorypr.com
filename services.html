Calculation of Value

A clear answer to the question: what is my business worth?

A defined-scope engagement performed under NACVA Professional Standards — the practical tool business owners and their advisors need for the decisions that don't require a formal opinion of value, but still demand rigor and credibility.

Ideal for planning, negotiations, and internal decisions where you need a credible number backed by professional standards. Not intended for litigation, regulatory filings, or financing where a formal opinion is required. For those situations, we can refer you to a trusted colleague offering comprehensive valuation services. Unsure which scope your situation calls for? Read our plain-English decision guide.

Common Use Cases

  • Buy-sell agreements
  • Succession planning
  • Internal decision-making
  • Early-stage negotiations
  • Periodic / recurring valuations
  • Tax planning support (consult your tax advisor for filing requirements)

Annual Update

Keep your valuation current without starting from scratch.

The second half of the AXIS Valuation Program: a scheduled annual refresh of your Calculation of Value using updated financials. A current, standards-compliant number should not be a one-time event — buy-sell agreements and exit plans are only as reliable as the valuation behind them, and that number ages.

Program clients receive their update on a scheduled annual cycle — same methodology, same rigor, faster turnaround, lower cost. Already hold an AXIS Calculation of Value? Updates remain available for engagements completed within the last 24 months.

Common Use Cases

  • Year-over-year value tracking
  • Partner equity reconciliation
