Our Core Service

Calculation of Value

When you need a credible, standards-compliant number. Fast.

A Calculation of Value is a defined-scope engagement performed under NACVA Professional Standards: we agree on the valuation approaches and procedures upfront, apply them, and deliver a written report — in English — documenting the value and the basis for it.

Appropriate for buy-sell agreements, succession planning, partner transitions, estate and gift planning, and internal decision-making. Which scope do you need? →
