Standards honesty

Calculation ≠ Conclusion

AXIS delivers Calculation of Value engagements and written Calculation Reports under NACVA Professional Standards. A Calculation is not a Conclusion of Value. AXIS performs the Calculation — not a formal Conclusion. Where a Conclusion engagement is required, AXIS will say so and refer appropriately — never bury the distinction.

Independence: AXIS is an independent advisory practice — not a CPA firm, and not paid referral fees for sending work elsewhere. Read Calc vs Conclusion →
