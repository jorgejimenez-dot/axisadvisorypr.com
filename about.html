Our Approach

Independence First

We have no financial interest in the outcome of any valuation. No brokerage commissions. No advisory fees tied to transaction value. Our only product is an objective, defensible number.

Built for the Local Market

We understand Puerto Rico's regulatory landscape, tax incentive structures, lending norms, and the dynamics of family-owned and closely-held operations. Our models are calibrated for the businesses that actually operate here.

Standards Without Excess

Scope is matched to the need — a focused Path A / PVA or an expanded Calculation of Value — without equating rigor with length alone.

Embedded, Not Competing

For CPA firms and advisors, we operate as an extension of your practice — not a competitor for your client relationships. Your clients stay yours. We provide the specialized analysis behind the scenes.

Professional Standards

Reference frameworks we work within.

NACVA Professional Standards

AXIS Calculation Engagements are developed and reported with reference to NACVA Professional Standards — the framework that guides how calculation work is scoped, disclosed, and presented in a Calculation Report. NACVA does not endorse AXIS as a firm.

AICPA SSVS-1

A valuation framework many CPAs already recognize. AXIS may describe its calculation work in terms familiar to advisors who work under SSVS-1; that does not mean AXIS claims AICPA membership, SSVS-1 engagement classification, or AICPA endorsement.

USPAP

The Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice inform ethics and competency expectations across appraisal disciplines. AXIS does not claim USPAP-certified appraisal practice or USPAP endorsement; any specific USPAP alignment language belongs in the engagement letter and report when established.

Independence isn't a limitation — it's the foundation of credible analysis. We answer to the standards and the data, not to a predetermined outcome.
