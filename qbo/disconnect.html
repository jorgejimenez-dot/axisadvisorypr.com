AXIS QuickBooks disconnect

If Intuit redirected you here after disconnecting AXIS from QuickBooks Online, the connection for this company should be treated as ended.

AXIS will discard or cease using access and refresh tokens for that connection and will not call QuickBooks Online APIs until you reconnect.

To reconnect, use the Connect / Reconnect page and complete Production OAuth again with Jorge’s Intuit credentials (MFA required).
